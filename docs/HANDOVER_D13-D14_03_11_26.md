## The logic in short
Shelf, wall cabinet, anti-tip: **wall type + size only, no weight question.**
1 Wall → plug type, hammer on/off, wall cap, max. depth · 2 Size · 3 App fits board / rail / kits · 4 Plug options 6–14 mm, each "max. X kg" · 5 Customer confirms.
- Fixing chain: plug size (= drill bit) → screw Ø → bracket hole / rail slot (screw Ø ≤ hole − 0.5 mm).
- Capacity = MIN(system × f, bracket pair or rail × f, board) ÷ 1.25, max. wall cap. Shelf f = MIN(1, 25 ÷ depth); cabinet f = MIN(1, height ÷ depth).
- Max. depth: shelf 50 / 35 / 30 cm, cabinet 60 / 40 / 35 cm (concrete, solid brick, wood / perforated brick / aerated concrete, plasterboard). Too deep → hint, not stop. Over 200 cm shelf → split or cut.
- Wall cabinet: weight = panel surface × 11 kg/m² (estimate); show only plugs ≥ 1.5 × weight; max. 80 kg (anchor bolts = Release 2).
- Anti-tip: depth ≤ 30 / 50 / 75 cm → 6 / 8 / 10 mm (M4 / M5 / M6); width → kits (1 per 90 cm); over 180 cm → next plug size + 2 fixings.
- Spirit level on every shelf and cabinet list: P032 (60 cm), P371 above 120 cm.
- Stops only: wall "I don't know", high-risk work, wall cabinet too heavy for the wall.
- Picture project keeps its weight question (D-2).
