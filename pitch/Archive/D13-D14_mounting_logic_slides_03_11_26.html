Plug decides the rest: plug size = drill bit → screw → bracket hole. One chain, no guessing.

Weight from the size: panels × 11 kg/m². The estimate is about 20 % above the real weight, so it stays on the safe side.

Bigger cabinet, bigger plug: the smallest plug offered carries 1.5 × the cabinet weight. Screws and rail follow.

Wall cap stays: bigger plugs can't make a weak wall stronger. Perforated brick 35 kg · aerated 40 kg · plasterboard 25 kg.

Depth decides the plug: up to 30 cm → 6 mm (M4) · 31–50 cm → 8 mm (M5) · 51–75 cm → 10 mm (M6). The wall decides the plug type.

Width decides how many: 1 kit per 90 cm, rounded up. 80 cm → 1 kit · 160 cm → 2 kits.

Tall furniture, stronger fixing: over 180 cm → next bigger plug and 2 fixings per kit.
